how a rule was arrived at rather than how to obey it: two spend-door designs that lost, the

part of the reasoning a reader has to have to follow it, stays exactly where it was; what goes is
the account of how the decision was reached. None of it is forgotten, because all of it was
already written outside the measured corpus before this branch: entries 0004, 0039, 0052, 0053 and
0076 carry each measurement in more detail than the deleted sentences did, and the tests that

suite reads any of the removed text; each of the three references was read from its first line to
its last, not as a diff, before a byte came out of it.

clothes, weighed by nobody, and a later author would spend a number no reader here approved.

measurement is 0102's own.
