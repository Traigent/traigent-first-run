stopping being described as something it no longer is. A fourth arm was added to the recommended
action so that a run owing the customer something with no ceiling attached could say so; three

The larger half is not the correction. It is the placement decision attached to it, which is a
product judgement and is recorded where the wording it governs lives. A hold that caps nothing is
carried where the card is explained, never at the approval to spend, and its routes run
continue-first. Reversing either turns a small nearly free run into an invitation to go away and
audit a whole answer key before starting - and the value of a first run is watching the machinery
work end to end, not certifying the material it works on. The safety reference now says why its own
card declines to carry that ask, so the omission reads as a decision rather than as a gap somebody
will helpfully fill later.

The byte weight sits in the two references because that is where both halves are decided and neither
is a flow mandate: the field gloss belongs beside the payload contract, and the ordering belongs
beside the check that raises the hold. The flow document gains nothing at all, which is the split
this ledger keeps asking for and rarely gets so cleanly.

Free, and worth naming because it is invisible in the arithmetic: the README carries the same two
corrections and is measured by nothing here. Its account of why a band sits below its number knew
only about thin measurement, and its account of what can ask you for something knew only about
