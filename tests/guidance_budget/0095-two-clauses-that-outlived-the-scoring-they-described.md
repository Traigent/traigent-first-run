Two sentences in `references/component-creation.md` described arithmetic that stopped being true.
One told an author that answering `"used": false` on the tools question removes that wiring check;
the other told them a check they could not settle leaves the pillar instead of scoring zero. Both
were accurate when written and both promised, between them, that an answer nobody can verify costs
its author nothing - which is precisely the incentive the scoring change beside this entry closes.
An unverifiable answer now keeps its weight and earns no credit whichever of the two shapes it
takes, so the first clause becomes "earns nothing here" and the second says what the weight does.
Neither sentence gains a rule; each loses a promise the reader would have been charged for
trusting.

Twelve bytes come back rather than going out, and the TOTAL ceiling comes down by the same twelve
so nobody quietly inherits them. Headroom under it is unchanged at fifty-seven bytes, which is the
figure the branches queued behind this one were sequenced against, and moving that number is a
decision none of them asked me to take on their behalf. RESIDENT and DOCUMENT are untouched: the
edit lives in one reference, and the largest single document is a different file entirely.
