Five watched runs of this guide opened on the decision. Each began with a
single line stating the choice - reply A or B, pick the walkthrough task -
placed above the greeting, the progress line, the readiness card and every
piece of evidence underneath it. The rules already here forbade anything
following the standing exit and were silent about what may sit in front of the
card, so a summary hoisted to the top broke nothing written down and five
independent runs each wrote one. Read that way round, a customer meets the
decision before the material arguing for it, and the card underneath reads as
backfill for a verdict somebody already reached. Placement is the whole repair
and placement cannot be pruned into an existing sentence: the ordering rule
buys its bytes in the operating contract, where the other presentation rules
already are, and again at each of the two places a run actually stops, because
a run reaches one stop and never the list of them.

The remaining bytes buy vocabulary at the same two stops. A gap was put to the
reader as a state of the inventory rather than as the things missing from it,
so a customer was told the search came back short and never told, in the three
nouns they use themselves, which of their dataset, agent or evaluation method
this run intends to supply. Beside that, a zero-anchor stop offered its task
choices as problem categories - extraction, classification - which name a shape
of work rather than a job anybody in a product actually has; put as the job,
"pull the total and the date out of a receipt" needs no decoding and can be
recognised or rejected on sight.

One sentence more earns its place because of what a project can already be
holding. Recorded request and response traffic is material a dataset can be
drawn from, and a stop that says only that examples will be written throws that
away in the customer's hearing. Naming the three pieces and the traffic they
come from turns an apology for what is absent into an offer built on what is
present, which is the same distinction this package draws everywhere else
between what a run looked for and what a project has.
