This buys prevention where the guide previously had detection. `algorithm="auto"`
with an unreachable managed brain does not fail: measured on a stub agent with no
provider call, it completes, returns two trials and a null portal link, and leaves
a fallback reason for somebody to read afterwards. The guide's instruction was to
treat that reason as a failure to investigate - true, and it arrives after twelve
paid trials the customer approved as a managed search. `TRAIGENT_REQUIRE_CLOUD=1`
turns the same condition into a raise before the first trial, and the paragraphs
here are what makes setting it safe rather than merely set.

Most of the length is the ways it does not work. The flag fails any run creating
no backend session, so the deliberately local baseline dies under an inherited
one and the wrapper removes it for that process - which is neutralizing something
the customer's environment asked for, so the baseline card names it instead of
doing it quietly. In the other direction either offline spelling resolves the run
to local-only before a session is attempted, the enforcement site never executes,
and the phase completes locally while the guard reads as on. That last state is
worse than never setting the flag, because the wrapper then looks protected, and
it is why the refusal is a loop over both names rather than one comparison.

Refusing rather than clearing costs a sentence and is the whole ethical content
of the change. No-egress says where somebody's data may go. A run that unsets it
to satisfy its own preference has answered that question on their behalf and sent
the data, which no amount of convenience earns; the honest move is to stop and let
them lift it or finish on the baseline, and the safety reference now carries that
question to the approval card where it costs nothing to ask.

The mock check is three lines and sits before the phase split because it is not a
connected-phase concern. Canned responses are free, report synthetic token counts
that slip past the wrapper's spend guard, and produce scores describing the mock;
a baseline measured that way poisons the comparison every later number is read
against, so both phases refuse it.

Owning the failure is the rest. A phase that stops instead of degrading hands
somebody an exception, and two shapes reach it with different bills - a session
that never opened spent nothing and leaves the baseline standing as the result,
while a brain lost mid-search has already bought trials that must be recovered and
reported as an early stop rather than dressed as a frontier. Naming both, and
refusing the obvious escape of re-running without the flag, is what keeps a
fail-closed run from arriving as a traceback.
