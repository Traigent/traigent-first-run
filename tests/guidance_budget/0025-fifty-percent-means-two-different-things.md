# 0025 - fifty percent means two different things

follows: 0024
total-ceiling: 327_400
total-measured: 327_063

The dominance line moved from a share of the rows to a distance above chance,
and the glossary entry describing it did not survive the move. It spelled out
one number - how alike two questions must be before they count as repeating -
and left the neighbouring question, whether a single answer has taken over,
with no figure whatever. That silence was tolerable while the rule was "nine
rows in ten", which needs no explanation because it says itself. Against chance
it is not tolerable, because the reader cannot arrive at the boundary from the
constant: a top answer on half the rows is the best a yes/no set can manage and
twice what guessing earns on a four-way one, and no amount of staring at "one
third" recovers that.

So the bytes buy the translation rather than the definition. Two shapes, both
sitting at half the rows, landing on opposite verdicts, next to the word that
separates them - which is the shortest form in which somebody holding their own
file can predict what this check will say about it before running anything.

They also buy the second regime, and that one is not decoration. Where the
answers are sentences rather than labels there is nothing to divide by, the
check declines, and a customer whose card shows this line blank needs to read
that it was skipped and not that it was cleared. Every other unmeasured result
in this package says so out loud; a new one that stayed quiet would be the same
defect wearing a newer rule.

The figure was restated twice, and both reasons are worth recording because
both are the defect the passage exists to prevent. A first draft closed by
saying free-text answers report as not checked, full stop, which is not what the
check does - an answer covering nine rows in ten is a ceiling whoever wrote the
rows, and is still flagged there.

The second restatement replaced the word free-text itself. Review found the
underlying rule classifying the customer's task and then telling them the
classification, wrongly, on ordinary label sets carrying rare labels. What the
run can honestly say is narrower: your answers are used by fewer than two rows
each, so their shares are a listing rather than a measurement. The sentence now
says that, and covers the small-label-set case it never had a word for.
