# 0056 - halt the spending when the portal stops listening

The connected-run rules promised in three places that a run whose tracking
breaks mid-flight stops buying trials at once, and that nobody would ever
finish paying only to be told afterwards that nothing had reached the portal.
Nothing enforced any part of it. Driven over the real door with a local
stand-in for the provider and no network anywhere: an exhausted approved total
does refuse a call, while a search stamped `local_fallback` and `rejected`, and
carrying no portal link, placed three provider calls across three trials with
two of them arriving after the SDK had already given up on tracking.

What the bytes buy is a read of published state rather than a detector of its
own. The pinned release marks a rejected or failed submission on the run's
configuration object, and the search binds that very object to the decorated
function - the same object, measured by identity on a live run whose own
session manager was made to flag itself, with the mark legible from inside a
`litellm.completion` wrapper on the call immediately after. That puts the halt
where every paid call is already weighed against the approved total, and one
blocking search offering no per-trial hook stops being a reason it cannot
happen.

A third of the promise turned out to be unkeepable and now says so. A portal
link is published on the object the search hands back, never during it, so no
gate reached from inside can notice an absent one; that reading now fires the
moment the search returns, ahead of held-out scoring, where before it waited
two hundred lines and one paid pass. The trial under evaluation when the
backend broke is billed too, because the flag goes up only once its result is
submitted - which the rules also state now, in place of a stop that cost
nothing.
