A prune, and the first entry in this chain to lower a ceiling. Eight bytes came out of
`sdk-execution.md`, which is also the file holding the DOCUMENT maximum, so the largest single
document shrank alongside the sum. The ceiling drops with the measurement rather than being kept
over a package that got smaller, because a kept number would read as headroom nobody decided to
grant.

What moved is one instruction out of the cell that carries its opposite. The knob catalog offers
nine controls under a heading saying seven of them are eligible, and two rows answered the question
"choose it when" by replying that the knob may not be chosen here at all. Both exclusions are right
and neither changed. Where they were written is what was wrong: an assistant scans a table and reads
the prose under it, so the row said do-not-pick in the one column reserved for pick-this, while the
heading three lines up already carried the true count.

So the two rows now wear their ineligibility in the name cell, their third column answers what the
control is actually for, and the reason travels down into the paragraph that was already the home
for "why this knob is not here" - the sentence excluding `batch_size` and `max_tokens` for moving
throughput without moving the answer. Naming the two knobs there is what keeps the marker findable;
a marker whose reason lives nowhere is a second puzzle rather than a fix.

Neither row leaves the catalog, and that is deliberate. Listing them is what stops an assistant
proposing retrieval or tool controls for a paid space this run cannot trace. The older test pins the
nine names against the SECTION rather than the table, so lifting a row out and leaving the name in
prose would have stayed green - measured, by deleting a row and re-running it. The new test counts
the rows and reads the third column, so both the removal and a third exclusion written into the
choosing cell are refused the first time somebody tries either.
