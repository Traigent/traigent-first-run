source as generated was capped, and an identical row that declared nothing was
not, so staying quiet scored better than telling the truth. Scoring silence as
generated needs two new rungs, and both rungs need their own explanation in

declaring lifts the ceiling only while the declared-generated rows are a
minority, and why half-collected-half-silent is capped by neither. That last
case is the one a reader will otherwise work out wrongly, and prose is the only
place it can be stated.
