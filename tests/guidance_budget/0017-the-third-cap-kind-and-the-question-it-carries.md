#203, stacked on #202. The scorer grew a third state some waves back and every
document that explains the taxonomy stayed at two, so a reader of this package
was told there is stopping and there is bounding, full stop. The state in
between is neither: nothing is broken, the comparison is worth paying for, and
a human still has to look at something first. Squeezing it into "stop" cancels
a paid run because an assistant did not like the look of somebody's rows.
Squeezing it into "bound" prints a ceiling with no way out, which is precisely
the shape that had to be repaired once already, one cap at a time, while the
prose kept describing the world before that repair.

Most of the bytes are not the taxonomy sentence. They are the thing the third
state exists to produce and never had. A flag whose only visible effect is a
slug in a JSON payload is not a remedy, so the middle kind now becomes an
actual question, put once, at the last instant it is free to answer: the
approval that already halts in front of the first billed call. What rides on
that halt is six items, each earning its line because leaving any one out
returns the reader to guessing - which shortfall was found, what got dropped in
its place, openable locations for both files, the two rows at either end of the
range so nobody judges the set by a lucky middle, a plain statement of what the
grader treats as right, and a fork with two named exits and no quiet default.
That last one matters most: agreeing to spend has never been the same act as
agreeing that the yardstick is yours, and until now the guide let one stand in
for the other.

Placement follows the rule this repository already keeps. Everything expandable
sits in the safety reference beside the approval it belongs to; the flow file
gets the trigger, the pointer, and the one prohibition a reader might otherwise
invent for themselves, that this is extra content on a halt already scheduled
and not a fresh interruption. Six-sevenths of the new prose therefore never
enters the resident half at all.

Three smaller repairs come with it and cost almost nothing: the marketing page
stopped promising that nothing will ever be asked of the reader, the closing
card in the safety reference stopped guaranteeing a `proceed` verdict that an
asking condition legitimately overrides, and a comment in the scorer stopped
describing a card line the space-sized evidence sentence retired.
