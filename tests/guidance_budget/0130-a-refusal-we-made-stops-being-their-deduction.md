Three hundred and eighty bytes, and every one of them is a sentence that stopped being true when the
scorer changed underneath it. traigent-first-run#507 is the decision that a check THIS GUIDE refused
on the customer's behalf is never their deduction: the run whose evaluator executes candidate code
or SQL was being marked `withheld` - which this scorer defines as "asked and did not answer" - on a
question nobody asked it. The declaration now retires that charge, so the evaluation pillar moves 31
to 59 on the fixture the suite pins, while the score a customer is graded on and the 45 ceiling stay
exactly where they were.

`references/run-safety.md` is the document that owned the wrong half. It said whether the check is
charged depends on what preflight's walk found, "the same witness decides both" - true when it was
written, and after #507 the last surviving statement of a deduction the scorer no longer makes. It
matters more than the others because the other two defer to it: `component-creation.md` and
`evaluation-and-dataset.md` both name it for which arm carries what. So the one decision had one
home, and the home was the file the behaviour change had not opened. It now says the bound depends
on the walk and the charge depends on nothing, with the corollary at the top of the file named as
the reason, because that corollary was already unconditional and this was the place it was still
being broken.

`references/component-creation.md` carried the compact version: passing `--calibration-scope-refused`
"moves no number". What replaced it names the numbers in both directions, and that took two passes.
The first replacement said "moves no readiness figure" - which is false of a readiness figure the
card prints, `Weighted average before caps`, and that one does move. A sentence right about the two
numbers a customer is judged on and silent about the one they can watch move is the same defect one
level down. Both documents now say the score and the band hold still and the pillar and the pre-cap
average rise, and they say WHY as ownership rather than as arithmetic: an assistant told only that a
number rises has been handed a reason to pass a safety declaration.

One word had to go, and the guard that found it was not looking for it. This repository says
"charged" for points, and `billing_ceiling_mandates` reads a sentence carrying a ceiling term and a
money term as a legislation about provider billing - so "the run stops being charged" beside "lifts
no ceiling" arrived as a second home for the walkthrough-ceiling mandate. The guard is right to be
crude there, and the sentence was ambiguous anyway: a customer reading "charged" next to "ceiling"
reads money, whatever this file means by it. It says "stops losing points" now, which is what it
meant, and the false positive was the thing that noticed.

The claim had a second home outside the guidance, and a test already knew it: the flag's own
`--help` said "and no number" too, and `test_no_document_says_the_scope_refusal_cannot_be_checked`
asserts both homes in one place precisely so neither can be corrected alone. It is not an
assistant-facing document, so it costs this ledger nothing - but it is read by the assistant
deciding whether to pass a safety declaration, which is the reader who matters here.

`references/evaluation-and-dataset.md` needed no change, and that is worth recording so the next
reader does not redo it: it speaks of `evaluator-calibration-refused` as an evidence boundary whose
bound is "on what the card may say and never on what the customer may do", and defers on which arm
carries it - arm-aware already, and so untouched by a change that moves only which arm is charged.
The pre-spend bullet in `run-safety.md` is unconditional by construction and says nothing about
deductions at all. Those two were checked in the first pass and the scope-gate disclosure twenty
lines from the pre-spend bullet was not, which is how the one home came to be the one file left
alone - the whole-document read is the rule for exactly this, and reading the passage the change is
about is not the same as reading the document.
