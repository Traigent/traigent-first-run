Both budgets move, by roughly a median each: RESIDENT by 405 against a recorded median of 500 over
49 raises, TOTAL by 1_343 against 1_342 over 81. That symmetry is not a coincidence and it is the
argument for the split that produced this entry. 0108 carried this paragraph once, and the pair of
them together was 545 bytes over RESIDENT and 2_886 over TOTAL - a single number nobody could weigh,
because two unrelated decisions were adding into it and neither could be judged on what it was worth.
Separated, each is an ordinary raise a reviewer can price against what it buys. The bytes did not
shrink; the question got answerable.

RESIDENT moves here where it deliberately did not in 0108, and the reason is that this one is a
mandate rather than an explanation. The close is a stage of the flow, and a stage that nobody orders
does not happen: the paragraph could sit complete in the reference and still never be reached,
because nothing in the ordered document sends a reader to it at the moment it applies. So two
sentences are bought in the expensive budget - one at the close naming the paragraph and its home,
one on the substitute-component line - and the paragraph itself is bought in the cheap one, read
once by whoever followed the pointer.

What it buys is the sentence a first run has never been able to say. A walkthrough that generated a
dataset, an evaluation method or an agent produces a real number over material nobody outside this
run has checked, and the guide had a vocabulary for the limitation and none for the value. A
customer who hears only the limitation concludes the hour bought nothing, which is false and is the
more expensive error of the two: they have results in the portal, a named next step, a feel for how
their easy questions differ from their hard ones, and a split held out of the search, which is the
habit every honest comparison after this one rests on. Both halves are mandated together because
either alone is the dishonest version - a warning with no worth is a run sold as worthless, and a
worth with no warning is a synthetic frontier sold as a production one.

The agent is named in that list and was named nowhere before. Dataset and evaluation method both had
their generated state priced and described; the third pillar this run can write had neither, so a
close could recite the limitations of two of the three and read as complete. Naming it costs a few
words in the reference and closes the one gap in the set that a reader had no way to notice was
missing.
