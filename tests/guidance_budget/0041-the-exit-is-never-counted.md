Two blinded runs numbered the standing `I have it` exit as a route. The rule
against it predates both, and both workers were following the flow. This entry
buys the two bytes-worth of changes that explain why.

The flow counted it. SKILL.md enumerated "two answers where something is missing:
proceed, or `I have it` and a path", so where material was missing the exit WAS
an answer - and the unusable branch then offers two routes with the exit on top
of them. A reader carrying the first count into the second produces build, I
have it, pause, which is what both runs produced. The reference counted it too:
its gap ask is one proposal plus the exit, described as two answers. The
document taught the defect it forbids elsewhere, which is why saying it again
would not have helped.

So the exit is never counted anywhere. One answer where material is missing, two
where it is only short, and the exit rides alongside every ask unnumbered and
last. Two pins that read the old ordinals moved with it rather than being
deleted: what they protect - that the exit is offered, that keeping what they
brought is named - is unchanged, and only the number moved.

The resident half is the other lesson, and it is the same one the opening
message's correction bought at 0038. SKILL.md named the routes and said nothing
about the exit, the lettering, or what may not appear, so the shape had to be
inferred from a reference loaded per stage. The shape is now stated where the
reader already is: letter from A, mark the build route recommended, close with
the unnumbered exit, and no route carries a decision of its own. The reference
keeps the worked example and the reasoning.

Resident rises to 79_350, past the previous ceiling. That is the decision
this entry records: a rule two careful readers missed twice is not a rule that
needs restating in the same place, it is one that is in the wrong place.
