Twenty-five bytes of RESIDENT against a recorded median of 500, and 965 of TOTAL against 1_342.
Both are well under one typical raise, and the split between them is the point rather than an
accident: the flow document gains three words naming a third thing the close owes, and the depth
behind those words is bought in the budget a reader pays once. The twenty-five buy the only part
that has to be carried every turn, which is that the obligation exists at all.

What it buys is a sentence the provenance fields cannot say. This run writes whatever the customer
is missing, and `SKILL.md` records "all missing components were built around the existing ones" as a
completion criterion - correctly, because it is the right way to build them. The consequence was
recorded nowhere. An evaluation method written around a dataset scores that dataset better than it
scores anything else, and the run then reports a number that method produced over that data; where
this run wrote both halves they agree by construction. Three nearby passages exist and none says it:
the provenance ceilings price each pillar's origin separately, the glossary warns about the same
rows appearing in both splits, and the reference's overfitting paragraphs are about the search
rather than about the ruler the search is measured with.

The held-out split is the reason this needed saying out loud rather than being left to a careful
reader. A held-out set is the standard answer to "how do I know this generalises", and it does
answer that for the search - but both of its sides are graded by the same evaluator, so it is silent
about an evaluator fitted to the corpus. A customer who has been told they have a held-out set and
has not been told this will reasonably conclude they are covered.

The second sentence is smaller and sits in the same place because it is the same omission. Where the
row review found unsound answers and the run repaired them, the repaired row's provenance flips
correctly and the score reflects it, and the customer learns that their data had a problem only by
reading a field they will not read. Saying it costs one clause.

Neither is a deduction, and the entry records that as a decision rather than an oversight. The
generated-provenance ceilings already price this material once. A second ceiling for the same fact
would charge twice for it, and the thing that was missing here was never a number - it was the
sentence.
