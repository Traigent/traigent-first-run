#149 splits one flag into two questions. "Is this true about your material" and
"does this stop you paying for a run" were the same boolean, so a walkthrough
dataset the guide itself writes closed the journey by demanding real data from
the only user who cannot have any. Separating them costs prose in SKILL.md,
where the per-condition routing list lives, and that list is resident by
necessity: it is what decides whether a stop is even offered, and a stop offered
late is worse than one never offered. Alongside it #159 records the severity
order beside the ceilings themselves, which the glossary has to restate for a
reader looking at a card.

The numbers matter more than either branch's. #149 weighed itself and wrote
231_000 against a measured 230_573. On this tree - with #159, #190 and #191 also
in - the real figure is 231_053, fifty-three bytes past what that branch would
have permitted, so taking its ceiling verbatim turns a correct merge red for
arithmetic neither author was in a position to do. Resident rises for the first
time in this ledger, from 61_129 to 62_152, which is why both budgets appear
here instead of one.
